# Product blueprint — revised synthesis

Prepared 2 November 2026. This document synthesizes the supplied master plan; it is not the missing original blueprint referred to in that attachment.

## Decision

Build a social-to-bookings workspace for independent Indian salons first. The salon niche is a planning choice, pending interviews. Restaurants and boutiques remain subsequent playbooks. Lead with: **See which tracked social activity led to a booking or recorded sale, and approve useful content in your language.**

Do not promise that every sale can be traced, that attribution proves causality, or that AI guarantees growth. Scheduling, AI and ROI reporting already exist in competing products. The proposed advantage is the combination of localized onboarding, Tamil/Hindi/English content, WhatsApp workflow, INR commerce and a transparent evidence ledger.

## Primary customer and job

The owner operates one location, posts inconsistently, answers Instagram and WhatsApp leads, and wants bookings. They need a daily shortlist: approve today's draft, follow up with leads, record a sale. A freelancer helping several owners becomes the second buyer only after the single-business flow retains customers.

The first session ends at a ready-to-approve draft. The first week should end at a tracked booking or clearly marked manual sale. The product must remain useful when publishing approval is unavailable: export the asset/caption, remind the owner, and let them attach a published URL.

## Pilot scope

Business profile, one niche playbook, three writing languages, owner approval, Instagram/Facebook capability-dependent publishing, manual fallback, lightweight lead list, tracked campaign reference, provider-linked payment record or manual sale, refunds, explainable revenue view, deletion/export, usage caps, audit trail. The pilot is a subset of P0–P4, not a claim to deliver all 82 launch features.

Defer YouTube, best-time AI, advanced media, creator marketplace, courses, memberships, enterprise listening, native apps, white-label and autonomous spending until demand and operational capacity justify them. Do not copy Whop's banking, cards, trading or financing products into the social SaaS.

## Validation and commercial gates (proposed targets)

Interview 10 salon owners; recruit 5 pilot workspaces; seek at least 3 paid commitments before expanding scope. Measure time to first approved draft, draft edits, weekly active owners, follow-ups completed, attributable/unmatched sales, time saved and renewal intent. At week four seek at least 3 of 5 owners using the app weekly and willing to renew. These are decision rules, not statistically significant product-market-fit evidence.

Try workspace subscriptions at ₹499/₹999/₹1,499 with explicit usage limits. Do not sell unlimited AI or messaging. Let owners compare the outcome and effort saved with the fee. Choose a single price after real usage measurement; do not launch three complicated segments immediately.

## Product rules

- Approval defaults to required. Editing an approved draft invalidates approval. Canceling queued work is different from retracting a delivered message.
- Every revenue amount has a source: provider confirmed, owner entered, or estimated. Refunds reverse entries; they never erase history.
- A user cannot merge social identities solely because names match. Ask for verified linkage or keep separate contacts.
- External text is untrusted input to agents. Tools, tenant scopes and spend checks are enforced by code.
- Missing permissions produce a useful manual flow. Connector errors are visible; fabricated live success is forbidden.
- Public sharing is opt-in and redacts contact data. English/Tamil/Hindi quality requires native-speaker review.

## Success metrics

Activation = profile confirmed and first approved draft. Value event = follow-up completed or sale recorded with source. Retention = workspace performs a meaningful action in successive weeks. Revenue metrics separate gross recorded sales, refunds, net recorded sales and subscription revenue. Track unattributed transactions rather than quietly dropping them. Track AI/messaging cost per activated and paying workspace and customer-support minutes.
